- Flow:
  1. ITP at 10 mG
  2. LBFGS polish with Sobolev `alpha=0.5`
  3. RTP under a time-dependent `B(t)` built from `TimeDependentZeeman` plus

  - Phase 3: `63 uG -> 0`, snap, `100 us`

  - `lbfgs_10mG_final_psi.jld2`
