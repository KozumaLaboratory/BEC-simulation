### B-1: implementer_sympy — compute initial-slope dF_z/dt|_{t=0+} + t* (Candidate C closed form)

*Issue*: T14 §4 Candidate C: the empirical τ_emp ≈ 7-14 ms is the time for *"clear asymmetry by t=2-4 ω⁻¹"* (memory:barnett_spin_pumping_observed_2026_05_16.md lines 30-32), while T11 §2.6's τ_cascade = 1/(W^CG_{+F} γ_dr) ≈ 78 ms is the *inverse top-rung rate*. These are different quantities. The clean closed-form question is: what is dF_z/dt|_{t=0+} under the rank-2 cascade ODE, starting from full m=+F polarization, given γ_dr = 0.02 and the full shape vector from T13 S6? The expected answer is dF_z/dt|_{t=0+} = -W^CG_{+F} · γ_dr · F (since only m=+F is populated initially, all Δm=-1 transitions out reduce F_z by 1) = -(13/14) · 0.02 · 6 = -0.0796 ω⁻¹. **t*** (time for ⟨F_z⟩/N to drop by 1) = 1/0.0796 ≈ 12.6 ω⁻¹ ≈ **18.2 ms** at ω_ref=691.15 rad/s. **This is within factor 2 of the empirical 7-14 ms** — much closer than τ_cascade = 78 ms. Wait — re-check: dF_z/dt|_{t=0+} should account for both Δm=-1 AND Δm=-2 transitions from m=+F. The rank-2 CG sum *already* includes both q=-1 and q=-2 (T13 sympy S1: `sum_{q in {-1,-2}}`). So W^CG_{+F} = 13/14 is the *total* normalized rate out of m=+F, but the Δm change is q-weighted: a transition to m=+5 (q=-1) reduces F_z by 1, a transition to m=+4 (q=-2) reduces F_z by 2.
