## F=6 biaxial nematic — recipe

Spinor: $\zeta^{(F=6, \rm BN)} = (|F=6, +2\rangle + |F=6, -2\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$.

Residual symmetry: $D_4$ (generated by $C_4$ about z + $C_2$ about x).

Apply Paper #3 §VII.A modified theorem (D_n axial dihedral, $n=4$):

$$
\varepsilon_{\rm LHY}^{(F=6, \rm BN)} = \frac{8\sqrt{M^3}}{15\pi^2\hbar^3}\,n^{5/2}\,\left[c_0^{5/2} + |\lambda_z|^{5/2} + 2|\lambda_\perp|^{5/2}\right]
$$

Sympy work needed: compute $c_0$, $\lambda_z$, $\lambda_\perp$ as rational
polynomials in $\{g_0, g_2, g_4, g_6, g_8, g_{10}, g_{12}\}$ (7 channels).

The recipe mirrors §VII.B (F=2 BN, same residual symmetry $D_4$):

1. **σ_m, δ_m for $\zeta^{(F=6, \rm BN)}$**:

   $\sigma_m = \sum_S g_S |\langle S, m | F=6, m_1; F=6, m_2 \rangle|^2$ summed over
   $(m_1, m_2)$ with the BN-state amplitudes.

   $\delta_m$: similar with the off-diagonal anomalous coupling.

   For $\zeta = (|+2\rangle+|-2\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$, the dominant pair channels are
   $|+2\rangle|+2\rangle$ (total $m_t=+4$), $|-2\rangle|-2\rangle$ ($m_t=-4$),
   $|+2\rangle|-2\rangle$ ($m_t=0$), $|-2\rangle|+2\rangle$ ($m_t=0$).

2. **BdG block-diagonalise under $D_4$**: 13-dim spinor decomposes into $D_4$
   irreps. Identify the phonon block (trivial irrep, gives $c_0$), the axial
   spin Goldstone block ($A_2$, gives $\lambda_z$), the transverse pair ($E$,
   gives $\lambda_\perp$ doubly), and the gapped blocks.

3. **Express stiffnesses as linear polynomials in $g_S$**: Each stiffness is
   $\Sigma_S \alpha_S^{(\cdot)} g_S$ with rational $\alpha_S$. The
   F=2 BN values (§VII.B) suggest 3-5 terms with manageable denominators
   (the F=8 octahedral §V.E has up to $\sim 10^6$ denominators).

**Expected output** (mirroring §VII.B):

$$
c_0^{F=6, \rm BN} = \alpha_0 g_0 + \alpha_2 g_2 + \alpha_4 g_4 + \alpha_6 g_6 + \alpha_8 g_8 + \alpha_{10} g_{10} + \alpha_{12} g_{12}
$$

with $\alpha_S$ ∈ ℚ. Same shape for $\lambda_z$ and $\lambda_\perp$.

**Estimated sympy work**: 2-4 hours per stiffness × 3 = 6-12 hours, plus
verification against a direct 26×26 BdG diagonalisation at a test config.

## F=6 cyclic_tetrahedral — recipe

The F=6 cyclic state in the codebase is built by `init_psi_cyclic` (state_zoo).
Its explicit spinor needs to be extracted to start; the residual symmetry is
expected to be $T_d$ (mirroring F=2 cyclic).

Paper #3 §V.A already handles F=2 cyclic at $T_d$. The F=6 cyclic could
inherit the same structural form $c_0^{5/2} + 3|\lambda_{\rm spin}|^{5/2}$
(polyhedral §III universal theorem, line 685 master table). If true, no
"modified theorem" needed; just sympy for the $c_0$ and $\lambda_{\rm spin}$
coefficients at F=6, mirroring §V.D (I_h at F=6) and §V.E (octahedral at F=8).

**Estimated sympy work**: 4-8 hours total (2 stiffnesses with single-orbital
structure simpler than BN's 3-stiffness D_4 case).
