# Profile of split_step_rotating! on Eu151 16³ DDI=100 shows
# apply_ddi_step_rotating! is 61% of total step time (2.1 ms / 3.97 ms).
# It wraps the baselined apply_ddi_step! (327 μs) with 2 _apply_UB!
# rotation round-trips (≈4 apply_uniform_spin_rotation! calls) and 2
# copyto!s — ~1.77 ms of pure wrapper overhead, not yet baselined.

apply_ddi_step_rotating!  kernel/apply_ddi_step_rotating_eu151_16cubed  Eu151 16³ rotating basis (theta=0.3, phi=0). 2.1 ms / 47 allocs / 73 KiB on the wrapper layer; underlying apply_ddi_step! is 327 μs (baselined separately) so the round-trip cost is ~1.77 ms. Optimization vectors: (a) short-circuit when theta=phi=0 and theta_dot=phi_dot=0 (lab basis ≡ rotating basis — common during ITP and Berry runs without B̂ tilt — skip the 4 rotations + 2 copyto outright); (b) fuse the 2 rotations per _apply_UB! into a single SU(D=13) rotation by composing exp(-iφ Fz)·exp(-iθ Fy) into one rotation matrix, halving the matmul work; (c) eliminate the copyto round-trip — call apply_ddi_step! directly on psi_tilde with rotated Q-tensor or pre-rotated spin matrices (full rewrite, larger scope). Watch: the existing kernel/apply_ddi_step_eu151_16cubed and kernel/uniform_spin_rotation_eu151_16x16x8 baselines pin the underlying primitives; any wrapper-level change must not regress those.
